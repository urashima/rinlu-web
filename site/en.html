02 / THIS IS RINLU

RINLU is a little musical universe filled with curiosity. Unexpected questions, feelings too precious to hide, and bright sparks from everyday life can all become songs.

Any style is welcome, as long as it has a little cuteness. With a playful streak and a soft heart, RINLU explores all that music can be—cheering you on as you leap ahead, or keeping you company as you quietly find your glow.
