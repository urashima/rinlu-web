Privacy Policy

How RINLU handles account details, Google sign-in information, and your music and gameplay data.

Effective: November 18, 2026

01

我们收集的信息

当您使用 RINLU 音乐与音乐游戏服务时，我们会根据您实际使用的功能处理必要信息：

  • 登录及账号信息：账号标识、登录方式，以及您授权提供的电子邮箱、显示名称和头像。
  • 音乐与创作内容：您主动提交的歌曲、封面、歌词、专辑、游戏配置、作品说明及相关审核记录。
  • 使用记录：收藏、播放历史、游戏进度与交互记录（以实际启用的功能为准）。
  • 技术与安全数据：为维持服务正常运行可能记录的 IP 地址、设备或浏览器类型、访问时间、错误日志和必要的 Cookie 或会话标识。

我们不要求您为浏览本隐私政策而登录。

02

Google 登录与授权

当您选择使用 Google 登录时，Google 会向您展示所申请的权限。RINLU 仅接收您同意授权并实际返回的身份信息，用于创建或关联账号、验证登录身份及保障账号安全。通常涉及 Google 账号的基本资料，例如姓名、邮箱、头像及账号唯一标识。

使用 Google 登录不等于授权 RINLU 读取您的 Gmail 邮件或 Google 云端硬盘文件。我们不会索取您的 Google 账号密码。如果未来需要新的 Google 权限，我们会在申请前说明用途并请求您授权。

您可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接设置 中管理或撤销授权。撤销后，依赖该登录方式的功能可能受到影响，但已存储的数据不会因此自动全部删除。

03

信息的使用目的

我们处理上述信息，是为了提供账号注册与登录、音乐播放、收藏与历史、创作者发布、音乐游戏、内容审核与版权管理、客服与安全防护，并改善服务的稳定性和体验。我们不会将通过 Google 登录获得的个人信息用于与上述目的无关的广告定向。

04

Cookie 与本地存储

网站可能使用必要的 Cookie、会话令牌或浏览器本地存储，维持登录状态、保存语言和界面偏好、执行安全检查。您可通过浏览器设置管理这些数据；禁用必要存储可能影响登录和部分功能。若未来使用非必要的分析或广告技术，我们会按适用法律提供相应告知或选择。

05

共享与服务提供商

为运行平台，我们可能委托云托管、数据存储、身份验证、网络传输和安全服务提供商，在提供服务所需的范围内处理信息。我们也可能在法律要求、保护平台及用户合法权益，或征得您同意时披露必要信息。

RINLU 不以出售个人信息为业务模式。我们要求处理服务数据的合作方按照适用法律及合同约定保护信息。

06

保存期限与跨境处理

我们仅在实现所述用途所需的期间内，或根据适用法律要求保存数据。账号注销或删除申请处理后，部分数据可能因法律义务、争议处理、反滥用与备份周期而在有限时间内保留，并在期限届满后删除或去标识化。

由于服务器或服务提供商的所在地，您的信息可能在您居住国家或地区以外进行处理；我们将依照适用法律采取合理的安全和保护措施。

07

信息安全

我们采用与服务规模相适应的访问控制、传输保护、会话安全及权限管理措施，并尽量限制工作人员和服务提供商对个人信息的访问。任何互联网服务都无法保证绝对安全；如发现疑似账号风险，请及时更改相关凭据并联系平台。

08

您的权利与数据删除

在适用法律允许的范围内，您可以请求访问、更正、导出或删除个人信息，撤回可撤回的同意，或申请注销账号。请通过本页末尾列出的平台官方联系入口提出请求。为保护账号安全，我们可能需要核验请求人的身份。

删除账号不一定会立即清除您已公开发布且被其他用户合法引用的内容；具体处理将依据内容性质、适用法律和平台规则进行。

09

未成年人

如您尚未达到所在地法律规定的独立同意年龄，请在父母或法定监护人的指导下使用服务，并在法律要求时先取得监护人同意。监护人如认为未成年人信息被不当收集，可通过官方联系入口提出查询或删除请求。

10

政策变更

我们可能因服务功能、技术或法律要求变化而更新本政策。重大变更将通过网站显著位置或其他适当方式告知。页面顶部展示最新生效日期；您可以随时重新查看。

11

如何联系我们

本政策适用于 RINLU 提供的音乐及音乐游戏服务。如需咨询隐私或提出个人信息权利请求，请通过 RINLU「关于与法律」 页面公布的官方联系方式联系平台运营方。

01

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, RINLU may process:

  • Account data: your account identifier, authentication method and profile information you authorize, such as email address, display name and profile picture.
  • Creative content: songs, artwork, lyrics, albums, game configurations, descriptions and associated moderation records you submit.
  • Activity data: likes, listening history, game progress and interactions, where those features are enabled.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser or device type, access times, error logs and necessary cookies or session identifiers to operate and protect the service.

You do not need an account to read this policy.

02

Google sign-in and consent

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shows the permissions being requested. RINLU receives only the identity information you authorize and Google actually returns, for creating or linking an account, authenticating you and protecting account security. This commonly includes basic Google account information, such as name, email address, profile picture and a unique account identifier.

Signing in with Google does not itself grant RINLU access to Gmail messages or Google Drive files. RINLU does not ask for your Google password. If additional Google permissions are needed in the future, we will explain the purpose and seek your authorization first.

You can review or revoke access in your Google account connections. Revocation may affect sign-in, but it does not automatically delete all data already held by the service.

03

How we use information

We use information to provide account access, music playback, collections and history, creator publishing, music games, content moderation, copyright management, support and security, and to improve reliability and the user experience. We do not use personal information obtained through Google sign-in for unrelated targeted advertising.

04

Cookies and local storage

We may use essential cookies, session tokens and browser storage to maintain sign-in, remember language and interface preferences, and perform security checks. You can manage them in your browser, although disabling essential storage may prevent some features from working. If we introduce non-essential analytics or advertising technologies, we will provide the required notices or choices under applicable law.

05

Sharing and service providers

We may engage hosting, storage, identity, network and security providers to process information only as needed to deliver the service. We may also disclose necessary information to comply with law, protect lawful interests or with your consent.

RINLU is not operated as a business that sells personal information. We require service providers handling service data to protect it in accordance with applicable law and contractual commitments.

06

Retention and international processing

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described or required by applicable law. After an account closure or deletion request, certain records may remain for limited periods to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or complete backup cycles before deletion or de-identification.

Depending on where servers and providers operate, information may be processed outside your country or region. We apply reasonable safeguards as required by applicable law.

07

Security

We use access controls, transmission protections, session security and permission management appropriate to the service, and aim to restrict personal-data access to authorized personnel and providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you suspect account misuse, update relevant credentials and contact the platform.

08

Your rights and deletion requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, export or deletion of personal information, withdrawal of consent where possible, or account closure. Use the official platform contact route below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Closing an account may not immediately remove content that you made public and that others lawfully referenced; the handling of that content depends on applicable laws and platform rules.

09

Children and minors

If you are below the age at which you may consent independently under local law, use the service with parental or guardian guidance and obtain their consent where required. Guardians who believe a minor's information was collected improperly may request access or deletion using the official contact route.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology or law. Material changes will be communicated through a prominent site notice or other appropriate means. The effective date appears at the top of this page.

11

Contact

This policy applies to music and music-game services operated under the RINLU name. For privacy inquiries and data-rights requests, use the official contact details published on RINLU's About & legal page.
